# UV Energize 2027 — working prototype

A prototype attendee app and year-round community site, built for PDS Equipment to look at.
Nobody has to install anything: **[open the demo](https://purplesharkdesigns.github.io/uv-energize-2027/)** and it runs in a browser, on a phone or a computer.

Built by John Barlow, Purple Shark Designs, after attending UV Energize 2026 in Nashville.
There is no charge attached to any of this and no invoice coming. If PDS wants it, it's theirs.

---

## What to press

The red bar across the top has two switches.

**Time of year** — Before / Show week / After. The site rearranges itself depending on when
you'd be opening it. During the show, the show tools come first. The rest of the year they
fold away and the community pages come forward. It's the same link and the same icon on the
phone either way.

**Demo view** — Attendee / PDS / Sponsor. Three different people see three different things
from the same site.

Worth a look in particular:

- **Community** — share a couple of machines under People first, then come back. Unanswered
  questions about gear you said you run get routed to you. That's the part that keeps a
  community alive rather than hoping people keep in touch.
- **Find a Printer** — six questions, ends on "talk to your rep", no price anywhere.
- **PDS view → Insights** — what people are shopping for, what the room already owns, and
  where the content gaps are.
- **Sponsor view** — the same demand data with names stripped out, as a boundary rather than
  a promise.

## Please read this part

**The data in here is invented.** The 2026 agenda and the reading list are real. Every
attendee, every entry, every sponsor, every community post and every restaurant write-up is
made up, so the demo has something to show without putting words in anyone's mouth.

**The printer specifications need checking.** They were read off Mimaki's public pages so the
tool would work. Nothing has been reviewed by PDS. Anything marked ⚠ is a figure that could
not be confirmed. The logic that decides what to recommend is a first guess and should be
rewritten by whoever actually sells these machines.

**Nothing is saved anywhere.** Everything you do in the demo lives in your browser for as long
as the tab is open and then it's gone. No accounts, no database, no data leaving your device.

## Why this is on GitHub

So it's open. PDS can see exactly what it is, hand it to anybody — including somebody other
than me — and none of it depends on me still being around. It seemed a better way to mean
"I'd build it and get out of the way" than a sentence promising it.

## What it's made of

One HTML file. No frameworks, no build step, no dependencies to install or keep patched.
Open `index.html` in any browser and it runs. That's deliberate: the fewer moving parts, the
less there is to inherit.

A real version would need a database and sign-in behind it, which is the main thing this
prototype doesn't have.
